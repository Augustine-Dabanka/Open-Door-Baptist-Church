What to Expect

Come As You Are

Walking into a new church can feel daunting — so here's exactly what your first visit looks like. Expect a friendly face at the door, heartfelt worship, a clear and encouraging message from the Bible, and people genuinely glad you came.

No dress code, no pressure, no awkward spotlight. Just come and experience the presence of God with a family that will welcome you like their own.

Your First Visit

Four Simple Steps

From the car park to the closing prayer, here's how your Sunday will flow.

1

Arrive & Be Welcomed

Come 10–15 minutes early. Our welcome team will greet you, help you find parking, and show you in.

2

Your Kids Are Safe

Check your children into our Children's Ministry, where they'll learn about Jesus in a fun, safe space.

3

Worship & the Word

Enjoy vibrant worship and a practical, Bible‑based message you can carry into your week.

4

Stay & Connect

After the service, say hello — we'd love to meet you, pray with you, and help you feel at home.
