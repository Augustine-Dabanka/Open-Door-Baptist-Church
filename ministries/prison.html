Prison Ministry

"I was in prison and you came to visit me." — Matthew 25:36

About Our Prison Ministry

Our Prison Ministry brings the message of hope, freedom, and salvation through Christ to inmates in correctional facilities. We believe no one is beyond the reach of God's grace — every soul matters.

Through prison visits, Bible studies, worship services, and aftercare programs, we walk with the incarcerated and their families on the journey of restoration.

Prison Visits

Regular visits to share the Gospel and pray with inmates.

Bible Studies

Weekly studies that bring God's Word behind the walls.

Family Support

Care and prayer for families left behind.

Aftercare

Reintegration support and discipleship after release.
