About Our Music Ministry

The music ministry exists to lead the church into the presence of God through Spirit-filled worship. Our choir, instrumentalists, and praise team blend skill and surrender to magnify the Lord.

Whether you sing, play, or simply love worship, there's a place for you to grow and serve.
