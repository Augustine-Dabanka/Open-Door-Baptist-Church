Women's Ministry

About Our Women's Ministry

Our Women's Ministry is a sisterhood where women are strengthened in their walk with God and encouraged to flourish at home, at work and in the church. Through prayer, the Word and warm fellowship, we build one another up in faith and grace.

Whether you're a mother, a wife, a professional or a young woman finding your footing, there's a place for you to grow, serve and belong.

Sisterhood

Warm friendships and support among women who love the Lord.

Bible Study

Growing together in the Word and in godly character.

Prayer

Interceding together for our families, church and community.

Outreach

Serving our community with compassion and the love of Christ.
