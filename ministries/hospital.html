Hospital Ministry

"Heal the sick... freely you have received; freely give." — Matthew 10:8

About Our Hospital Ministry

Our Hospital Ministry brings the comfort of Christ to the bedside of the sick and suffering. We pray with patients, encourage families, and stand with medical staff as they care for the wounded and weary.

Through visitation, prayer, Scripture, and practical care, we declare that no one walks through illness alone — God is near, and so are we.

Bedside Visits

Personal visits to bring prayer and the peace of Christ to patients.

Prayer Teams

Standing in faith for healing — physical, emotional, spiritual.

Family Care

Walking with caregivers and loved ones during hard seasons.

Bibles & Tracts

Distributing the Word of God to those who need hope.
