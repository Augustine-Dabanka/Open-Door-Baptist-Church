About Our Media Ministry

Our Media Ministry uses the tools of today — cameras, sound systems, livestream, and social media — to broadcast the timeless message of the Gospel. We capture moments, preserve sermons, and reach the world from Kumasi.

If you have a passion for tech, design, video, or storytelling, this is your ministry.
