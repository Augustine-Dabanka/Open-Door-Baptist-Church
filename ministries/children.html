About Our Children's Ministry

Our Children's Ministry is a safe, joyful place where kids learn the truth of Jesus through stories, songs, games, and Scripture memorization. We partner with parents to nurture little hearts toward a lifelong relationship with God.

From toddlers to pre-teens, every age group has age-appropriate teaching led by trained, loving volunteers.
