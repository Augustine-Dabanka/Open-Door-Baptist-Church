Who We Are

Open Door Baptist Church is a Christ-centered family in Kumasi, Ghana — Welcome to Open Door Baptist Church; a place of refreshing change. A place where sickness cannot thrive. A place where members are accident-free. This is Jesus Christ's Church!

Founded on the unshakable Word of God, we are committed to worship, discipleship, and the proclamation of the Gospel to the ends of the earth.

Our Vision

To be a place of refreshing change where members are transformed by Christ to lead change in their communities and spheres of influence.

Our Mission

To help members discover and experience God's transforming purpose for human life and just live it.
