Who We Are

Open Door Baptist Church is a Christ-centered family in Kumasi, Ghana — a place where the wounded find healing, the lost find purpose, and every soul encounters the love of God.

Founded on the unshakable Word of God, we are committed to worship, discipleship, and the proclamation of the Gospel to the ends of the earth.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of God's love, raising disciples who transform their world for Christ.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, equip believers, and serve our community with compassion and truth.

Our Values

Faith, family, integrity, service, and unwavering devotion to Jesus Christ.

About the Church

Open Door Baptist Church is a place of refreshing change, where memories are made and souls are transformed.
